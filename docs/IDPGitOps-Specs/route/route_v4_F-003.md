## Guardrails

- A gate reads committed state only; never reach a live cluster or a vendor API.
- A new gate is wired into both pre-commit and CI, or the asymmetry is recorded.
- The rule a gate enforces is stated in prose beside it.
- Curated lists over heuristics where a false negative is expensive.
- A new gate ships with a test, or the gap is recorded as an open question.
- Treat everything under `docs/IDPGitOps-Specs/` as read-only while implementing; a shape that does not fit is a spec bug to report.
- Status cells move through `docs_flow.py task`, one legal transition at a time, and a `done` cites a check that ran here.
- One task at a time, in the order the task file gives.
